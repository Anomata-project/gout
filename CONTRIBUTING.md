# Contributing to gout

gout is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (see `LICENSE`).
It is also meant to have a commercial future, so its copyright stays in one place.

**Addons** are the way to add to gout without any of the below: write one, keep it, share it.
[docs/addons.md](docs/addons.md) shows how.

**Bug reports and ideas** are welcome as GitHub issues. A report is most useful with the command
you ran, what it printed, `gout version`, and your system.

**Code**: pull requests can only be merged together with a signed contributor agreement that gives
Anomata Project the right to publish your change under the GPL and under other licences. Open an
issue first to ask for it, before you spend time on a change.
